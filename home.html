About Me

I am a first-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student who is continuously learning and exploring the world of technology. As a beginner in this field, my college journey has taught me many new things and given me opportunities to develop my knowledge and skills.

In our Introduction to Computing course, I learned different practical skills and activities, such as creating object detection projects using Roboflow, making and testing Ethernet cables, installing operating systems, and designing websites using HTML and CSS. These activities helped me understand how technology works and how it can be applied in real-life situations.

Meanwhile, in our Introduction to Programming course, I experienced challenges, especially when learning how to write code, understand programming concepts, and solve errors. There were times when programming felt difficult and confusing, but through practice, patience, and determination, I gradually learned how to create programs and improve my coding skills.

Being a first-year IT student is not always easy, but every lesson and activity gives me a chance to learn something new. I believe that making mistakes is part of the learning process, and every challenge helps me become better.

I know that I still have a lot to learn, but I am excited to continue this journey, explore more about technology, enhance my skills, and prepare myself to become a successful IT professional in the future.
